About us

The Turk Boutique Hotel offers a unique blend of modern amenities and classic charm. Our dedicated staff is committed to providing exceptional service to ensure your stay is memorable.

We are a small, family-owned hotel located in Durban. We take pride in taking care of our customers and providing them with a warm welcoming experience.

Our hotel is based on the strong unity and welcoming experience of Turkiye

Mehmet Citlak is the founder and owner of The Turk Boutique Hotel. With a background in hospitality and a deep appreciation for Turkish culture, Mehmet is dedicated to providing guests with an authentic and memorable experience.
